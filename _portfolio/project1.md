This planned publication will explore compact molecular representations for antimicrobial resistance (AMR) research. The work will investigate how dimensionality reduction can retain meaningful molecular relationships while making complex data easier to analyse.

The research aims to provide a foundation for efficient molecular analysis and machine learning.
